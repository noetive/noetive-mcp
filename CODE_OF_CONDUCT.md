# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Everyone participating in this project — issues, pull requests, discussions — is expected to be respectful and constructive. Assume good faith, critique the work rather than the person, and accept that people have different levels of context.

Unacceptable: harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, publishing others' private information, and sustained disruption.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces, and when someone is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report a problem to **conduct@noetive.io**. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, and may temporarily or permanently block anyone whose behaviour they judge inappropriate; where the reason is not obvious, they will explain it.

Maintainers who do not uphold this in good faith may face the same consequences as anyone else.

Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
